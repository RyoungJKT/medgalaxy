## 0. Constitution amendments

The motion constitution (original section 4) is amended in four places, and nowhere else. Each amendment exists because a client note requires it; nothing here is a general loosening.

**A1. The world spring gains an analytic form.** The world family is a critically damped spring. A node flying along a curved path cannot be integrated as a spring per axis without leaving the path, so the same curve is now also available in closed form:

```
arrival(x) = (1 - e^(-5x) * (1 + 5x)) / (1 - 6 * e^(-5))
arrival(0) = 0 exactly, arrival(1) = 1 exactly
```

This is the critically damped step response with time constant d/5, normalized to land. It is not a new easing, it is the sanctioned one written down. Only the assembly fly-in and the Time Machine's entry and exit blends may use it.

**A2. A third sanctioned overshoot: the year-step settle.** Peak magnitude 4.5 percent, duration 240 ms, at most three nodes per detent crossing, only inside the Time Machine. `back.out(1.2)` peaks at 5.29 percent, so the year-step settle is strictly quieter than the detonation and the supernova pop, and the hierarchy of the two original overshoots is preserved by construction. Form: one half sine, `m(t) = 1 + A * sin(pi * t / 240ms)`, which returns to exactly 1.000 at t = 240 ms.

**A3. Travel beats join camera beats and the morph in the over-700 ms exemption.** The original allowed durations above 700 ms only for camera beats and the morph. Add: the assembly fly-in (per node, up to 3.25 s), the Time Machine exit blend (1.10 s), the entry blend (650 ms, already sanctioned), and the long-leg sweep (1.30 s). Node travel is world motion and mass takes time; this is the same principle the morph already claimed.

**A4. Ambient micro-motion is exempt from the duration table.** Camera breathing, star shell rotation, edge shimmer and node breathe are continuous oscillators, not transitions. They are governed by amplitude and frequency, stated in section 4, and by one rule: no ambient channel may exceed 1.0 percent of the quantity it modulates, and every one of them stops the instant a directed stillness is called (beat 2's ignition hold, the detonation push-in, any active fly).

Sanctioned durations are unchanged: 120, 180, 240, 320, 480, 650. The staircase's 360 ms year is a composite of two of them (240 travel plus 120 dwell), not a new constant.

## 1. Ending restage: the film ends at home

### The defect

The shipped sequence is film, then tour, then a permanent park. `TimeMachine.jsx` ends the tour by setting `tmPhase` to `'scrub'`, holding the finale's isolation and caption, and 2.5 s later printing "Scrub the decades." Nothing ever releases it. The viewer's last unattended frame is a dimmed galaxy under an instrument chip, which reads as "the demo ended here" rather than "the instrument is yours." The client is right, and the fix is not a smaller park, it is an exit.

### The rule

The opening sequence ends at the home screen. Every automatic path (film to release, film to tour to finale) terminates in: `tmPhase: 'idle'`, papers sizing, the rest camera seat, no isolation, no tour caption, full chrome, the galaxy turning. The Time Machine lives in the header from then on.

One exception, stated so nobody removes it: a tour the viewer interrupted is not a park. If any input hands the scrubber over mid-tour, the viewer owns the rail and it stays up until they close it. The exit sequence below runs only when the tour reaches its own end untouched.

### The exit choreography

Clock `t` starts when the finale's closing shot has held for `FINALE_HOLD = 2.6 s` after the flatline cue (this replaces `FINALE_HANDOVER = 2.5` and the "Scrub the decades." chip, which is deleted from the automatic path entirely). Total exit: 2.60 s.

| t (s) | Channel | Action |
|---|---|---|
| 0.00 | store | `stopTimeMachine()`. `tmPhase` to `'idle'`, `tmFocusIdx` to -1, `tmCaption` to null, `storyVisible` restored |
| 0.00 | caption | flatline card exits on the standard 200 ms fade, no rise |
| 0.00 | isolation | `HighlightSystem` dim releases over 480 ms; `fx.glowSuppress` 0.55 to 0 over the same 480 ms, sine.inOut |
| 0.00 | sound | moment 3 pad swell, reused at -4 dB against the film's own |
| 0.10 | rail | `TimeRail` slides down and out, 240 ms expo.out; year numeral, ember dot and reticle leave with it |
| 0.15 | radius | `tm.exit` 0 to 1 over **1.10 s** (was 0.40), per node staggered by the existing lag factors: `staggeredEase(tm.exit, L[i])`. Thirty-five years collapse back into the whole record, giants landing last |
| 0.15 | camera | glide from the finale seat to `SEAT.rest` (1.0 R0, azimuth -15, elevation 8) over **1.60 s** on `easeGlide` with `RESIDUAL = 0.22`. The last 300 ms arm the velocity-matched handover exactly as the film's release does |
| 1.25 | grade | `fx.ember` confirmed at 1, ember rims re-assert on the overlooked decile |
| 1.30 | chrome | story chip row fades in, 240 ms, 40 ms stagger. Header, filter bar and legend are already up from the film's release and do not re-animate |
| 1.60 | hints | `orbit` and `select` chips return if undismissed. `timeMachine` is force-dismissed: the viewer just watched it |
| 1.75 | header | one-shot Time Machine button pulse, 1.4 s: two cycles of scale 1.000 to 1.060 to 1.000 and border opacity 0.35 to 0.90 to 0.35. Under it, a 9px `#64748b` micro-line, "the decades live here", up 2.6 s then a 200 ms fade |
| 2.60 | | exit complete. The galaxy is turning at `REST_ROTATE_SPEED` 0.3 and the first drag continues it |

The second handover must be indistinguishable from the first. Same `easeGlide`, same residual, same `sceneRefs.handover` arming. This is not a code convenience, it is the point: the piece hands control over twice and both times the frame the user touches is already moving.

**Skip during the exit.** Any input fast-forwards to the landed state over 240 ms: radii snapped through a 240 ms ease, camera tweens killed and seated at rest, chrome up immediately, button pulse cancelled.

**Reduced motion.** No glide, no blend, no pulse. Three 300 ms dissolves (caption out, isolation out, radii home), camera jumps to the rest seat, and the header button shows a static ring for 2.6 s instead of pulsing. Every caption and both state changes survive.

### Header re-entry, and how it differs from the old park

The old park left the viewer inside the finale: dimmed field, tour caption, the tour's compounded camera. Re-entry is the opposite of that. It is an instrument opening, not a story resuming.

`tmTourSeen` is already true, so `toggleTimeMachine` calls `startTimeMachine(false)` and lands in `'scrub'`. On top of the current behavior:

- **The camera does not move.** The viewer keeps their framing. No `camera-home`, no fly. This is the single largest difference from the auto-park and it is what makes re-entry feel like a tool.
- **Entry blend.** New `tm.enter` channel, 0 to 1 over 650 ms, per-node staggered by the same lag factors, using `arrival()` from amendment A1. The mirror of the exit blend. Today entry is a hard swap on the frame `startTimeMachine` runs, which is the one ugly cut left in the piece.
- **Rail in.** 240 ms expo.out from the bottom, playhead parked on the last year, one detent pip on the numeral.
- **One chip, 2.6 s, runtime derived:** `drag the rail. <first> to <last>.` with both years read from `data.yearStart` and `data.yearStart + data.nYears - 1`. No captions, no focus isolation, no narration.
- **The replay affordance.** A 9px `#64748b` text button at the rail's left end, "replay the decade story", calling `startTimeMachine(true)`. This is the only route back into the narrated tour, and it exists because a live demo needs to run the story twice without a page reload.
- **Closing again** runs the same 1.10 s radius blend home, no camera glide (the viewer owns the camera by now) and no button pulse.

### Sequence budget

Landing dismissal to home: assembly 5.20 + film 16.50 + tour arm 1.50 + tour 30.30 + exit 2.60 = **56.10 s**, against today's 55.35 s to a parked scrub. The restaged tour (section 2) is 3.05 s shorter than the shipped one, which is what pays for the longer assembly and the exit. Beats 1 to 3 are untouched at 16.50 s, still inside the 16 to 20 s spec window.

## 2. Cinematic year-scaling

Three changes, in descending order of effect: the curve gets more range, the tour stops lerping and starts ratcheting, and the top movers get accents. The first two are the honest mapping doing more work. Only the third dramatizes.

### 2.1 The curve

Round 6 fixed the ratio and left the absolute size on the table. HIV's 1990 to 2014 arc now reads 2.25x, but it travels from radius 3.13 to 7.02 inside an 18-unit ceiling, and the median cell in the whole table is radius 1.63. Most of the galaxy is too small for a real change to be a visible change. Ratio was the round 6 acceptance criterion; **absolute travel in radius units** is the one that answers this note.

In `src/utils/timeMachineData.js`:

| Constant | Shipped | New | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| `MXY` | 18 | **26** | the whole per-year domain gets 45 percent more range. This is the biggest single lever and it costs nothing in honesty: it is a uniform scale |
| `KNEE_SHARE` | 0.38 | **0.42** | the bulk (90 percent of cells) gets 42 percent of the range |
| `BULK_EXP` | 0.85 | **1.00** | below the knee, radius is exactly proportional to that year's count |
| `KNEE_PCT` | 90 | 90 | unchanged, still data-derived at build time |
| `MIN_RY`, `ZERO_RY` | 0.25, 0.05 | unchanged | |

Tail stays linear from the knee to `maxYearly`. Curve stays continuous at the knee and strictly increasing across the whole domain.

Measured against the real table:

| | shipped | new |
|---|---|---|
| HIV/AIDS 1990 to 2014 | 3.13 to 7.02, **2.24x**, travel 3.89 | 4.22 to 11.10, **2.63x**, travel **6.87** |
| HIV across the 1990 to 1996 tour leg | 3.13 to 4.96 | 4.22 to 7.34 |
| COVID-19 2019 to 2020 | 0.69 to 14.13, 20.6x | 0.68 to 20.75, **30.5x** |
| COVID-19 lead over 2020's number two | 1.111x, gap 1.42 units | 1.102x, **gap 1.92 units** |
| Rheumatic heart disease, 35-year span | 0.47 to 1.03, span 0.56 (3.1 percent of ceiling) | 0.44 to 1.10, span 0.66 (**2.5 percent of ceiling**) |
| median non-zero cell | 1.63 | **1.93** |
| nodes moving 0.15 units or more per year step, mean | 33.8 | **48.7** |

Every invariant holds and two improve. COVID's detonation is still the single largest year-over-year change in the table by both ratio and absolute delta, and by a wider absolute silhouette margin than it has ever had (1.92 units clear of pneumonia, against 1.42 today), even though the ratio lead moves 1.111 to 1.102. Rheumatic heart disease's flatline is proportionally flatter than it shipped. The new ceiling of 26 sits below half of the cumulative view's own ceiling of 55, and below that view's 90th percentile radius of 23.96, so the Time Machine still never looks bigger than the galaxy it sits inside.

**Where the honesty line sits, exactly.**

1. The mapping is monotone in count over the whole domain, zeros excepted at the floor. Equal counts give equal radii. The settled frame is always exactly the mapping. These are unit-tested and stay unit-tested.
2. `BULK_EXP` is capped at 1.00 forever. At 1.00 the sentence "below the knee, a node's radius is proportional to that year's paper count" is true and printable. Above 1.00 it is not, and no amount of drama buys that.
3. The cumulative view's own mapping (`nR`, `nRM`, square root, area-proportional) is untouched. The two curves answer different questions: the cumulative view compares diseases, the Time Machine tracks one disease through time.
4. **Disclosure keeps pace.** The methodology panel currently describes only the cumulative curve, and was truthful by omission. It is no longer allowed to be. Add a subsection titled "Time Machine size mapping" naming `src/utils/timeMachineData.js`, and stating the knee percentile, the knee count, the sub-knee exponent, the share, and the ceiling, all read from the built table at runtime rather than transcribed. One paragraph. It is the price of the extra drama and it is cheap.

### 2.2 The tour ratchets instead of lerping

The second reason year changes do not read: the tour never rests on a year. A leg is one continuous eased tween from pause year to pause year, so intermediate years are motion blur with no stable frame to compare against. Thirty-five years go by and the eye never gets a before and an after.

Replace `STEP = 0.65` continuous legs with a **staircase**:

- **One year = 240 ms travel (expo.out) + 120 ms dwell = 360 ms.** The dwell is what makes the change legible; it is a still frame at a real year.
- **Legs of 8 years or fewer** are pure staircase.
- **Legs longer than 8 years** are a sweep then a staircase: the first `S - 6` years travel continuously over **1.30 s** on sine.inOut with all accents suppressed, then the last 6 years ratchet at 360 ms each. A rush of years, then an arrival that ticks. During the sweep the year numeral drops to 55 percent opacity with a 0.6px blur and rolls continuously, restoring at the first stair.
- **Single-year legs keep 650 ms.** The 2019 to 2020 detonation keeps `back.out(1.2)` and its own push-in unchanged; 2020 to 2021 keeps its weight.
- **The rewind** (last year back to first, at the tour's open) stays 1.30 s with accents suppressed.

Resulting tour, against the shipped one:

| leg or hold | shipped | new |
|---|---|---|
| rewind | 1.30 | 1.30 |
| 1990 to 1996 (6 years) | 3.90 | **2.16** staircase |
| 1996 to 2019 (23 years) | 3.90 | **3.46** = 1.30 sweep + 2.16 staircase |
| 2019 to 2020 | 0.65 | 0.65 |
| 2020 to 2021 | 0.65 | 0.65 |
| 2021 to 2024 (3 years) | 1.95 | **1.08** staircase |
| six holds | 21.00 | 21.00 |
| **total** | **33.35** | **30.30** |

Shorter and far more legible, which is the whole trade. During manual scrub nothing changes: the critically damped 120 ms spring stays, drag stays analog and continuous between detents.

### 2.3 Accents

Accents dramatize. They are transient, they return to identity, they are invisible in an at-rest frame, and none of them can change which node is bigger.

**Gates, all four required:**

- **G1.** Accents fire only on integer-year crossings.
- **G2.** Suppressed whenever `|d(year)/dt| > 4.0` years per second. This kills the rewind (26 yr/s), the sweep phase (13 yr/s), and any hard flick, while allowing every staircase step (2.78 yr/s) and every deliberate scrub.
- **G3.** At most three accented nodes per crossing, ranked by `|delta radius|` for that step, each requiring `|delta radius| >= 0.25` (the 78th percentile of all 5,202 per-step deltas).
- **G4.** Tier budget: HIGH 3 nodes, MEDIUM 2, LOW 1.

**Accent 1, the ghost shell. This is the hero accent.** On each accented node, a sphere at the node's centre held at **the radius it had in the year just left**, category color, `MeshBasicMaterial`, transparent, `depthWrite: false`, normal blending (never additive: the bloom threshold stays reserved for the ignite ramp). Opacity 0.30 to 0, linear, over **480 ms**. The ghost never scales. Growth reads as a node breaking out of its old shell; shrinkage reads as a node falling inside it. It renders the year-over-year delta as a visible geometric difference rather than asking the eye to remember a frame from 360 ms ago, which is the actual perceptual problem. Cost: a pooled `InstancedMesh` of 8 slots, LRU recycled, one draw call, no shader. It runs on LOW.

Under the new curve the top-3 gate is met on all 34 steps, so every single year of the scrub has three nodes visibly shedding a shell. That is the client's note answered directly.

**Accent 2, the mover ring.** Reuse `fireRipple(idx, color)` from `SelectionRipple` on the rank-1 mover when `|delta radius| >= 1.50`. Color says direction: category color on growth, tertiary ink `#64748b` on shrinkage. Never `#ff4d1a`, which belongs to the detonation and the overlooked-decile scar.

This threshold fires on 9 of the 34 steps, and the years it selects are, mechanically: 2004 (obesity), **2009 influenza**, 2010 influenza, **2014 Ebola**, **2016 Zika**, 2020, 2021, 2023 and 2024 (COVID's rise and decay). Eight of the nine rings in the entire scrub land on an outbreak or its aftermath. Nobody authored that; the data did. Do not tune the threshold away from it.

**Accent 3, the year-step settle.** Amendment A2. Half-sine scale multiplier, 240 ms, peak 4.5 percent (rank 1), 3.0 percent (rank 2), 2.0 percent (rank 3), starting on the frame the detent lands. Returns to exactly 1.000. Below the detonation's 5.29 percent, so the hierarchy holds.

**Accent 4, the numeral pip.** Existing 1-frame 4 percent brightness pip stays. On a step where the rank-1 delta clears 1.50, it becomes 2 frames at 8 percent. It ties the rail to the field.

**Accent 5, the mover micro-label.** On the rank-1 mover only, only when the ring fires, and only during a step whose dwell is at least 360 ms: a 9px micro-line beside the node in the existing `NodeLabels` style, in the rail movers chip's own format, for example `Influenza +7,412 papers`. In 180 ms, holds 650 ms, out 240 ms. Both numerals are the difference of two file values, which is the precedent the rail's hover chip already set. Across the whole 35-year tour this fires at most twice (2023 and 2024, inside the final staircase); everywhere else it is silent.

**Reduced motion.** Ghost shells become a single 300 ms opacity dissolve with no fade curve, rings and settles and micro-labels are dropped entirely, the staircase collapses to stepped year holds as it does today.

## 3. Fly-in assembly: beat 0 restaged

The original beat 0 directed nodes streaming in along faint filaments and it was deferred. The shipped assembly is `IntroSequence`'s staged scale-up from zero, which is the exact pop-in the "no pop-in" rule was written against, four times over. The client is asking for the original beat, and the original beat was right.

**Budget: 4.0 s to 5.2 s.** New total from landing dismissal to release end: **21.7 s** (5.2 assembly + 16.5 film). Under the 5.5 s ceiling.

### Spawn

Every node's seat is its category-layout position `P_i` from `store.catPos`. Its spawn:

```
S_i = P_i * K_i + D_c * camDist * 0.85
K_i = 3.4 + 1.8 * h(i)
h(i), u(i) = two deterministic hashes of the instance index
```

`D_c` is one of **ten unit vectors, one per category**, placed on a Fibonacci sphere and clamped to elevations between -25 and +55 degrees so no stream enters from directly behind the camera. Deterministic hashes, not `Math.random`, so the assembly is byte-identical on every load and the verify harness can seek it.

Result: spawn distances between roughly 3.4 and 5.2 times the layout radius plus the category offset, every one of them outside the opening camera's near field and inside a 6 R0 shell. Ten streams, not 153 darts.

### Path

Quadratic bezier, `B(p) = (1-p)^2 S + 2(1-p)p C + p^2 P`, with

```
C = (S + P) / 2 + N * 0.22 * |S - P|,   N = normalize(cross(P, D_c))
```

Because `N` comes from the category's own entry vector, every node in a stream curves the same way. Ten ribbons spiralling in, which reads as matter falling into a galaxy rather than as a swarm. `p` is driven by `arrival()` from amendment A1: a critically damped arrival, no bounce, mass visible.

### Timing

```
stream launch:   0.16 * c seconds, c = 0..9 in legend order   (last stream at 1.44 s)
per-node start:  t_i = 0.16 * c(i) + 0.30 * u(i)              (max 1.74 s)
per-node flight: d_i = 2.10 + 1.15 * sqrt(r_i / r_max)        (2.10 s to 3.25 s)
```

Earliest arrival 2.10 s. Latest arrival **4.99 s**, and it is the largest node in the galaxy. The last thing to land is the biggest thing, and then 210 ms of stillness before beat 1 speaks. That silence is the beat.

### Per-node treatment in flight

- **Radius**: 0.55 at launch to 1.00 over the last 30 percent of the flight, smoothstep, no overshoot.
- **Comet stretch**: non-uniform instance scale along the travel axis, `s_long = 1 + min(1.8, v_i / v_ref)` with `v_ref = 0.9 * camDist` per second, decaying to exactly 1.000 by `p = 0.92`. The instance quaternion aligns to the velocity direction, one `setFromUnitVectors` per node per frame, **zero extra draw calls**. This is motion blur bought with a matrix. It is the only place in the piece where a node is not a sphere, and it is finished before any caption exists. Every quaternion must be identity on beat 1's first frame, and that is a test.
- **Brightness**: 0.35 at launch to 1.00 at landing, plus a 180 ms 1.30x pip on the landing frame. HIGH and MEDIUM ride a shader channel; LOW rides `instanceColor`, since LOW has no custom shaders. Same apparent result on all three tiers, and the 375x812 shot is the check.

### Filaments and dust

- **Filaments**: one `LineSegments`, 153 segments, one draw call, 306 vertices rewritten per frame. Each segment is a tangent tail running back along the node's own bezier by 0.12 of the remaining path. Color `#1b2740`, opacity `0.25 * min(1, v_i / v_ref)`, so fast nodes trail and landed nodes do not. Dead at beat 1. All tiers.
- **Dust**: HIGH only. The existing 400 background particles drift inward by 3 percent of their radius over the 5.2 s with a 0.4 percent per second rotation bump, easing back to the resting rate by 5.6 s. The whole volume settles. Zero new objects.

### Camera

Opening seat **2.9 R0** (was 2.2), elevation 12 degrees. Drift to 1.5 R0 across the full 5.2 s, sine.inOut, plus a **2.5 degree azimuth counter-drift** turning against the streams' curl so the ribbons sweep across frame rather than at it. `CameraRig`'s existing `runDrift(4.0)` becomes `runDrift(5.2)` from the new seat.

### Composition with the grade

Unchanged and deliberately so. `desat = 1` for the whole of beat 0; the category palette fades up over beat 1's first second exactly as today. The fly-in is monochrome, which is precisely what makes the color arrival the reward. Nothing about the ignite ramp, the suppression, or the bloom rule moves.

### First-frame integrity, honored harder

All 153 nodes exist in the first painted frame, at their spawns, at 0.55 radius, monochrome. Nothing appears from nothing at any point in the piece. Acceptance: at t = 0.00, instances with scale > 0 equals 153, and instances inside 2.0 R0 equals 0.

### Skip and reduced motion

- **Skip during assembly**: the existing 0.5 s power2.out fast-forward to the beat 1 seat stands, and now also force-lands every node over the same 0.5 s on the same `arrival()` curve. A skip never leaves a node in flight and never leaves a quaternion non-identity.
- **Reduced motion**: unchanged from today. `IntroSequence` skips the assembly outright; all nodes are seated on the first frame at full radius, monochrome, and the beat is a single 300 ms brightness dissolve. The reduced viewer is not made to watch stillness.

### Sound

Moment 1 is rewritten: the granular bed fades in over the first 1.0 s, each of the ten streams adds one partial as it launches (a rising ten-note cluster across 1.44 s), resolving to the single soft consonance as the last giant lands at 4.99 s, with one low thud at -18 dB on that landing. Still muted by default; still synthesized, still zero asset weight.

## 4. More motion, everywhere it is cheap

Ranked by wow per unit of cost.

**1. Camera breathing on every hold.** The single loudest tell of a canned demo is a perfectly static held frame, and this piece has eleven of them (three film holds, six tour pauses, scrub at rest, idle). An additive camera offset applied after all tweens, exactly like the existing cursor parallax and killed by `OrbitControls.onStart` exactly like the handover: azimuth ±0.45 degrees at 0.055 Hz, elevation ±0.25 degrees at 0.083 Hz, radius ±0.6 percent at 0.037 Hz. Incommensurate frequencies, so it never visibly repeats. Suppressed during beat 2's ignition stillness (that stillness is directed and must stay absolute), the detonation push-in, and any active fly. Cost: three sine calls per frame. This one change is worth more than the next three combined.

**2. Star parallax, three shells.** Split `CFG.particles` 0.30 / 0.45 / 0.25 across radii 2.8 / 4.0 / 6.2 times `camDist`, rotating at 0.00090 / 0.00040 / 0.00015 rad/s, sizes 2.2 / 1.5 / 1.0, colors `#3b4a63` / `#334155` / `#232f42`. Depth becomes readable from motion alone, which is what makes an orbit feel like space instead of a turntable. HIGH additionally gets a per-point twinkle through a small points shader (`sin(time * rate + phase)`, opacity 0.55 to 1.0). MEDIUM keeps `pointsMaterial` and gets parallax only. LOW's particle budget is 0 and stays 0. Cost: two extra draw calls of points on HIGH.

**3. Tour leg choreography.** During every staircase leg the camera trucks 4.0 degrees of azimuth and dollies in 3 percent, both sine.inOut across the leg, releasing the dolly at the pause. During a long leg's sweep phase the truck goes to 9 degrees and the dolly to 6 percent, so the fast-forward has motion under it rather than being a pure numeral change. The existing per-pause camera cues (HIV 0.80 and 0.62, detonation 0.85, peak and finale recenters) are unchanged and still win. Cost: one extra `flyTarget` per leg.

**4. Resting galaxy micro-breathe.** Per-node scale ±0.8 percent at 0.10 to 0.16 Hz driven off the `aPhase` attribute the geometry already carries, written into the matrix compose that already runs every frame. HIGH and MEDIUM only. Off during the Time Machine (year deltas must not be muddied by ambient scale), during beat 2, and on any selected node. Cost: literally zero, it is one multiply inside an existing loop.

**5. Edge shimmer during the film.** A luminance wave travelling the layout edges outward from the galactic centre during beats 0 and 1, so the filaments the nodes arrive along stay alive after the nodes land. HIGH and MEDIUM get a per-vertex phase in the edge shader; every tier gets the fallback, a global edge opacity breathe from 0.06 to 0.13 at 0.2 Hz. Film only. The hover neighborhood's existing 0.08 to 0.28 rise is untouched. Cost: one uniform. Ranked last only because the edges are faint by design and the ceiling on the payoff is low.

Performance envelope for all five combined: two draw calls on HIGH, one on MEDIUM, zero on LOW. The 60 fps desktop HIGH target and mobile playability are unchanged, and the fly-in's own additions (one `LineSegments`, one pooled ghost `InstancedMesh`) bring the total new draw call budget to four on HIGH, two on MEDIUM, one on LOW.
