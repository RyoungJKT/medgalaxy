CompareCards redesign, six directions

The boxes that appear beside a selected node. Same sample content in every design: selected node Cervical Cancer, a higher-mortality comparison (Sepsis) and the strongest research link (Obesity). Both cards stay clickable, jumping to that disease.

House rules honored in all six: no colored edge stripes, hairline borders only, status by dot or tint, solid-color type, IBM Plex Mono, sources one glance away.
